# Test Results

## Test Scenario

A YouTube video URL was supplied to the Smart Content Summarizer workflow.

## Expected Result

The workflow should process the video and produce a clear summary highlighting the key ideas in a readable output.

## Observed Result

The workflow successfully generated a structured webpage summary titled **The 5:1 Ratio**.

### Key Points Returned

- **The 5:1 Ratio** — stable and happy relationships maintain a minimum of five positive interactions for every one negative interaction.
- **Negativity Bias** — negative moments can carry greater psychological weight than positive moments.
- **Conflict Management** — successful couples use repair and positive reinforcement rather than simply avoiding conflict.
- **Predictive Power** — the material presents the ratio as a relationship-success indicator based on research associated with Dr. John Gottman.

## Result

**Pass** — the workflow produced the intended structured summary output from the test video.
